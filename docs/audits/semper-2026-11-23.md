The recorded TomTom route crosses the PU Marunda point, the PU Semper point (feature 194), and the geocoded Semper 1 plaza, then joins Jalan Syech Nawawi Al-Bantani. TomTom ends its TOLL section before the refund plaza.

The operator explains that Marunda collects JTCC plus JORR, refunds JORR when exiting via Semper to the Cakung–Cilincing arterial, and does not charge JORR again for continuing traffic:

The historical article's rupiah amounts are not used. The net JTCC fare comes from the current directed operator table, Gabus–Marunda (fixture Rp43,000). The displayed note distinguishes net cost after refund from the balance needed at Marunda.

The rule requires the named exit, a TOLL endpoint within 500m of PU Semper, a prior unique Marunda passage, and passage within 60m of Semper 1 in the first 500m after the endpoint. It does not globally alias Semper to Marunda. Unknown or continuing JORR exits do not receive this refund rule.
